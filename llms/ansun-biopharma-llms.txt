# Ansun Biopharma

> Privately held, clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in San Diego, California, founded in 2003. Ansun develops host-directed immuno-biologic therapies from a recombinant sialidase platform; its lead candidate DAS181 is a multi-function sialidase fusion protein studied in parainfluenza virus, severe influenza, human metapneumovirus and COVID-19. Ansun publishes no public API, SDK, developer portal or machine-readable specification — this profile is a company record, not an API record.
